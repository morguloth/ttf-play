Take the Field

A campaign of thirty battles

Three generals take the field

You start with six generals drawn at random, and each battle three are dealt to command it: some teach your troops a formation, others boost your soldiers, and a rare few multiply your whole battle score. Officers hold a tile of their own and multiply every soldier of their type around them. Win new generals and promote the ones who carried you through.

30 battles · 3 of 6 generals each battle · best on iPad, upright
